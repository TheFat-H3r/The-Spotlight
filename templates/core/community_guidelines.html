Community Guidelines

Standards that keep The Spotlight trustworthy, safe, and welcoming for everyone.

1. Honest & Accurate Listings

Only list products and services you legitimately offer. Use real photos, truthful descriptions, and accurate availability.

2. Respectful Communication

Treat customers, vendors, and support staff with respect. Harassment, spam, hate speech, or abusive messages are strictly prohibited.

3. Genuine Reviews

Leave reviews based only on real experiences with a store, product, or service. Fake or manipulated ratings are not permitted.

4. Prohibited Items & Reporting

Illegal, unsafe, or counterfeit items are banned from The Spotlight. If you spot a listing or account breaking these rules, report it through Contact Support.
