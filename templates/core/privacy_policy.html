Privacy Policy

How The Spotlight collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account or storefront on The Spotlight, we collect your full name, email address, phone number, role (Customer or Vendor), optional profile photo, and order or booking history.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used to authenticate your account, verify your email, connect buyers with vendors for orders and bookings, and send essential account notifications.

3. Session Cookies & Preferences

We use essential session cookies to keep you signed in and store your display, currency, and notification preferences.

4. Your Controls

You can edit your profile details, adjust privacy preferences, or deactivate your account at any time in Account Settings.
