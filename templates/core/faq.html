FAQ

Quick answers about shopping, booking services, and becoming a vendor on The Spotlight.

Finding and ordering items from local and campus vendors is simple:

  • Search by keyword or filter by category, max price (MUR), or location on the marketplace.
  • Open a product card to check details, pricing, and vendor information.
  • Add items to your cart and complete your order flow.
  • Coordinate pickup or delivery details directly with the store.

You can set up your business presence in a few steps:

  • Select Become a Vendor from the marketplace or footer.
  • Create your account or sign in and complete your vendor application details.
  • Add your store profile, business info, and products or services.
  • Start connecting with customers and managing incoming orders or bookings.

Service providers list their starting prices and availability across stores:

  • Browse services on the marketplace or visit a specific vendor's storefront.
  • Review service details and starting rates in MUR.
  • Click Book Now to request your preferred time or session.
  • Check your booking status and updates in your account.

Everything account-related lives inside Account Settings:

  • Update your name, email address, phone number, and profile avatar.
  • Change your password or review active login sessions.
  • Customize notification toggles, display preferences, and privacy options.

If something doesn't go as expected, our team is ready to help:

  • Note any order or booking ID related to your request.
  • Visit Contact Support and describe the issue clearly.
  • We'll follow up using the contact details you provide.
